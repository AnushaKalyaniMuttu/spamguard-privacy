SpamGuard Privacy Policy

Effective date: 1 October 2026

SpamGuard ("the app") is published by Anusha Kalyani Muttu. Contact: anushakalyanimuttu@gmail.com

Data the app accesses

  • Call log (READ_CALL_LOG, WRITE_CALL_LOG): to show recent calls and block unwanted callers. The app keeps a copy of your call history (number, contact name, call type, date, duration) in a database on your device.
  • Contacts (READ_CONTACTS): to show contact names next to numbers and to add numbers to Contacts.
  • Phone (CALL_PHONE): to place calls from the dialpad.
  • Notifications: to tell you when a call is blocked.
  • Call screening: if you make SpamGuard your caller ID and spam app, it checks incoming numbers against your blocklist.
  • Data you enter: your blocklist rules and, optionally, your phone number. The phone number is not verified.

How the data is used

Only to provide the app's features on your device: blocking calls, showing call history and statistics, and backup and restore.

Where the data is stored

On your device. The app has no servers, no accounts, no analytics and no advertising, and it does not send your data to the developer.

Backup to Google Drive

If you choose Back up, the app saves a file (spamguard-backup.json) to the location you pick in the system file picker, such as your own Google Drive. The file contains your blocklist, blocked calls, call history and the phone number you typed. It is not encrypted. Google handles files in Drive under Google's own privacy policy. The developer has no access to this file.

Sharing

The app does not sell or share your data with third parties. The only transfer is the backup file you choose to save yourself.

Retention and deletion

Data stays on your device until you delete it or uninstall the app. Uninstalling does not delete a backup file you saved to Drive. You must delete that file yourself.

Children

The app is not directed at children.

Changes

We will post changes to this page and update the effective date.
